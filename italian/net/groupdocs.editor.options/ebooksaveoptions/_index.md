1. [ePub](https://docs.fileformat.com/ebook/epub/) (Pubblicazione elettronica)
2. [MOBI](https://docs.fileformat.com/ebook/mobi/) (MobiPocket)
3. [AZW3](https://docs.fileformat.com/ebook/azw3/) (Formato Kindle 8t)
